Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-30

Grove is a small, independently-run chat app. This page describes what data it stores and why, in plain language.

What Grove stores

  • Account info: username, email address, and a bcrypt hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Content: messages you send, reactions, pinned messages, and channels/DMs/friends you create.
  • Session: a JWT stored in your browser to keep you signed in.
  • Presence: whether you're currently online, kept only while you're connected.

What Grove doesn't do

  • No third-party analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No selling or sharing your data with anyone.
  • No advertising.

Cookies & local storage

Grove uses browser storage only to hold your session token and a few local UI preferences — nothing that identifies you to a third party.

Who can see your data

Messages you send in a channel are visible to other members of that channel, same as any chat app. Direct messages are visible only to you and the recipient. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Deleting your account

You can permanently remove your account's personal information (username, email, password) from Settings at any time. Since messages are shared content — other people's conversations reference them — message text isn't force-deleted along with your account, but it's no longer linked to any identifying information about you.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contact@grove.example
